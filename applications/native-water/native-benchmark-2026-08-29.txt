Measurement boundary
  Every CPU and Vulkan run consumes the same retained unordered-pair graph.
  CPU time is one optimized single-threaded density-and-force coefficient pass.
  Vulkan kernel time is the device timestamp spanning density and force passes.
  Synchronized Vulkan time includes command encoding, submission, timestamp
  readback, and waiting for completion. Graph construction, GPU setup/upload,
  integration, collisions, coefficient rebuild after motion, and rendering are
  outside those pass times and are reported or excluded explicitly.

Interpretation boundary
  This establishes a machine-specific Vulkan speedup over this example's
  optimized one-thread Rust evaluator for the already-built coefficient graph.
  It is not a full-frame fluid speedup, an optimized multicore/production-SPH
  comparison, or evidence that the Native Space compiler discovered or emitted
  this graph and shader. The Rust and WGSL are handwritten translations of the
  retained-coefficient representation.
