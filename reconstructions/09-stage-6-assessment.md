## Open analytic completion

- a norm or topology on that carrier;

- convergence criteria for infinite character sums;
- Fourier series, Fourier integrals, or a Mellin integral;
- Euler products, zeta functions, analytic continuation, or zero theory.

operation domains, and camera continuity before an infinite expression can be
assigned theorem status.
