# Reconstruction Ledger

This ledger records established mathematics re-derived in Native Space terms.
A reconstruction is not a novelty claim. It passes Stage 6 only when it names
the known result, states its assumptions, gives a native derivation, translates
back to conventional notation, and assesses whether the native route is
shorter, more general, or merely equivalent.

## Comparison labels

- **Invariant-revealing:** the native proof isolates a reusable operation or
  conserved quantity more clearly than the expanded formula.
- **Structurally shorter:** after all previously proved dependencies are
  counted, the native proof removes substantive steps rather than hiding them.
- **More general:** the native statement applies on a strictly larger stated
  domain and specializes to the conventional theorem.
- **Equivalent reformulation:** the proof is exact but has no established
  simplicity or generality advantage.
- **Negative reconstruction:** the native route is longer, less clear, or
  requires equivalent hidden machinery; this is retained as evidence.

These labels may be combined, but every claimed advantage must identify what
was counted. Reuse of an earlier lemma can shorten a later derivation without
making the total foundational proof shorter.
