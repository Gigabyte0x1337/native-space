# R-DFT-1: Finite DFT Convolution Theorem

**Status:** Reproduced  
**Known result:** the discrete Fourier transform maps cyclic convolution to
pointwise multiplication  
**Dependencies:** L-ROOT-1, L-ORTH-1, T-CYCLIC-1, T-DFT-1, T-DFT-2  
**Native invariant:** native depth addition becomes cyclic depth addition under
an explicit quotient, then character evaluation turns that convolution into
pointwise MULTIPLY

## Conventional statement

For length-$N$ sequences $f,g$, let $*_N$ denote cyclic convolution and
let $\mathrm{DFT}_N$ use the root $e^{-2\pi i/N}$. Then

$$
\mathrm{DFT}_N(f*_Ng)
\mathrel{=}
\mathrm{DFT}_N(f)\cdot_{\mathrm{pt}}\mathrm{DFT}_N(g).
$$

The DFT is invertible.

## Native translation chain

For one primitive direction $k$, native MULTIPLY first performs ordinary
nonnegative depth convolution. C-CYCLIC-1 then folds absolute depth modulo
$N$:

$$
F\star G
\xrightarrow{\ \mathcal C_{N,k}\ }
(\mathcal C_{N,k}F)*_N(\mathcal C_{N,k}G).
$$

C-DFT-1 evaluates that cyclic coefficient vector against the native
orientation characters generated by

$$
\omega_N=\mathcal E_{\mathbf J}(-2\pi/N).
$$

T-DFT-2 proves

$$
\mathcal F_N(f*_Ng)
=\mathcal F_N(f)\boxtimes_{\mathrm{pt}}\mathcal F_N(g).
$$

The proof is finite distributivity plus the character law
$\omega_N^{a+b}=\omega_N^a\boxtimes\omega_N^b$. L-ORTH-1 proves character
orthogonality, and T-DFT-1 uses it to establish the exact inverse.

## Information accounting

The DFT itself is bijective and loses no cyclic coefficient information. The
preceding native-to-cyclic camera is lossy:

$$
n\varepsilon_k
\quad\text{and}\quad
(n+N)\varepsilon_k
$$

fold to the same residue. T-CYCLIC-1 describes every fiber by equality of the
aggregated residue coefficients. Therefore the composite transform cannot be
called equivalent to the original absolute-depth state unless a restricted
representative domain or the quotient relation is stated.

## Back-translation

T-FLOW-2 and T-CX-2 send $\omega_N$ to
$e^{-2\pi i/N}$. Native coefficient ADD and MULTIPLY become conventional
complex addition and multiplication, so C-DFT-1 and T-DFT-2 become the usual
finite complex DFT and convolution theorem exactly.

## Comparison

**Classification:** operations-first and equivalent to the finite group-
algebra proof.

The native chain makes a useful distinction that is often implicit: ordinary
depth convolution belongs to the native monoid, cyclic convolution is created
by a modulo-depth quotient, and the DFT is a lossless camera after that
quotient. This clarifies where aliasing enters. The proof is otherwise the
standard finite character proof and establishes no new theorem.

## Executable cross-check

[`../language/runtime/tests/reconstructions.rs`](../language/runtime/tests/reconstructions.rs) checks
the theorem on 100 deterministic exact-rational vector pairs for $N=4$,
where $\omega_4=-\mathbf J$ is exactly representable. This is an
implementation cross-check, not the general proof.

## What is not established

- No FFT implementation or $O(N\log N)$ runtime result is provided.
- No compression follows; a bijective DFT retains all $N$ cyclic
  coefficients.
- No infinite Fourier transform, sampling theorem, or anti-aliasing result is
  implied.
