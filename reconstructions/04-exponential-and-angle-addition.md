# R-EXP-1: Exponential and Angle Addition from Native Flow

**Status:** Reproduced  
**Known results:** real exponential addition law and trigonometric angle-
addition formulas  
**Dependencies:** A-ODE-1, L-FLOW-1 through L-FLOW-3, T-FLOW-1, T-FLOW-2,
T-CX-2, T-POLAR-1  
**Native invariant:** generator ADD becomes native flow MULTIPLY

## Conventional statements

For real $\rho,\sigma,\theta,\phi$,

$$
e^{\rho+\sigma}=e^\rho e^\sigma,
$$

$$
\cos(\theta+\phi)
=\cos\theta\cos\phi-\sin\theta\sin\phi,
$$

$$
\sin(\theta+\phi)
=\sin\theta\cos\phi+\cos\theta\sin\phi.
$$

## Native derivation

C-FLOW-1 first defines $\mathcal W_N$ from the ODE generated by native
MULTIPLY, without naming exponential or trigonometric functions. T-FLOW-1
proves operations-first that

$$
\mathcal W_N(\rho+\sigma,\theta+\phi)
=\mathcal W_N(\rho,\theta)
 \boxtimes
 \mathcal W_N(\sigma,\phi).
$$

This follows because parameter ADD is generator ADD and L-FLOW-3 turns
generator ADD into pointwise native MULTIPLY. T-FLOW-2 then identifies the
flow coordinates with

$$
\mathcal W_N(\rho,\theta)
=(e^\rho\cos\theta,e^\rho\sin\theta).
$$

The identification uses only the initial-value characterizations in A-ODE-1;
it does not assume the addition formulas being reconstructed.

## Back-translation

Set $\theta=\phi=0$ in the native homomorphism and compare first
coordinates. This gives

$$
e^{\rho+\sigma}=e^\rho e^\sigma.
$$

Set $\rho=\sigma=0$, expand D-OS-3, and compare both coordinates. This gives
the cosine and sine angle-addition formulas exactly.

Under the complex camera T-CX-2,

$$
\kappa(\mathcal E_{\mathbf J}(\theta))
=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta,
$$

so the same flow is the conventional unit complex exponential when that
exponential is characterized by the initial-value problem $u'=iu$,
$u(0)=1$.

## Logarithmic inverse boundary

T-POLAR-1 supplies the complete conventional fibers after T-FLOW-2 identifies
the two cameras:

$$
W^{-1}(z)
=\left\{
\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\log\nu(z),\theta_z+2\pi n\right):n\in\mathbb{Z}
\right\}.
$$

The scale coordinate is the inverse of the $\mathbf1$-generated flow. The
orientation coordinate is only defined modulo $2\pi$, and zero has no finite
flow parameter. Therefore a global, single-valued native logarithm on all of
$\mathbb{O}$ does not follow and is not claimed.

## Comparison

**Classification:** invariant-revealing and operations-first; equivalent in
analytic strength.

Unlike the earlier T-POLAR-2 proof, this derivation does not use exponential or
angle-addition formulas to prove that wrapped multiplication is parameter
addition. It derives that law from linear-flow uniqueness and native ring laws,
then obtains the familiar formulas as coordinates. That closes a genuine
circularity risk in the reconstruction order.

The total proof is not shown to be shorter than standard ODE or power-series
proofs. A-ODE-1 carries ordinary finite-dimensional analytic strength, so this
is not a derivation of analysis from pure algebra.

## Executable boundary

No 1.0 executable cross-check is recorded. Exact rational runtime values
cannot generally represent $e^\rho$, $\sin\theta$, or $\cos\theta$.
Adding floating-point samples without a precision and error contract would
weaken rather than strengthen the evidence.

## What is not established

- No global logarithm or zero preimage exists.
- No infinite native power series has been defined.
- No arbitrary noncommutative exponential law follows.
- No computational advantage follows from the flow coordinates.
